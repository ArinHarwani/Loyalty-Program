1. 🌟 What is LoyaltyQR?

LoyaltyQR is a WhatsApp-based loyalty program built specifically for Indian retail shops. Instead of expensive apps or printed punch cards, LoyaltyQR lets you run loyalty campaigns where your customers earn rewards through their WhatsApp — the app they already use every day.

💡 Why LoyaltyQR?

  • No app download needed — customers use WhatsApp, which they already have
  • QR-code based — simple, fast, and works offline on printed QR
  • Automated messages — progress updates, birthday wishes, expiry reminders
  • Works for any shop — restaurants, sweet shops, salons, clothing stores, and more

2. 🔄 How It Works — The Big Picture

1️⃣

You Create a Campaign

Set a goal (e.g., "Spend ₹5,000 in 30 days") and a reward (e.g., "Get 10% off next purchase").

2️⃣

Customer Makes a Purchase

You enter the bill amount in LoyaltyQR and a QR code appears on your phone screen.

3️⃣

Customer Scans the QR

The customer scans the QR from their phone, enters their WhatsApp number (only once), and taps "Confirm on WhatsApp".

4️⃣

Automatic WhatsApp Update

The customer instantly receives a WhatsApp message showing their progress: how much they've spent, how close they are to the reward, and how many days are left.

5️⃣

Reward Claimed!

When the customer reaches the goal, they get a unique claim code on WhatsApp. They show you the code, you verify it, and give the reward.

3. 🚀 Getting Started (Step by Step)

Step A: Sign Up

1Open the website

Go to the LoyaltyQR website and click "Get Started Free" or "Merchant Login" in the top right.

2Create your account

Click "Sign Up", enter your email address and a password (minimum 6 characters). Click "Sign Up".

3Set up your shop

On the next screen, enter your Shop Name (e.g., "Sharma Sweets") and select your Category from the dropdown (Sweet Shop, Restaurant, Cafe, Salon, Clothing Store, etc.). Click "Create My Shop".

4Wait for activation

You will see a "Pending Activation" screen. The LoyaltyQR team will review your details and activate your account within 24 hours. You will receive confirmation via email/WhatsApp.

Step B: Create Your First Campaign

1Go to your Dashboard

Once activated, log in and you'll see your dashboard. Click "+ Create Campaign".

2Choose campaign type

Spend & Win (Amount-based): Customer must spend a certain amount (e.g., ₹5,000) within a time period.
Visit & Win (Visit-based): Customer must visit a certain number of times (e.g., 10 visits) within a time period.

3Set the details

• Campaign Name: e.g., "Summer Special"
• Target: Amount (₹5,000) or Visits (10 visits)
• Duration: Choose preset (15, 30, 45, 60 days) or pick a specific end date
• Reward: e.g., "10% off next purchase" or "Free mithai box"
• Max Winners: (Optional) Limit how many people can win

4Launch!

Review the preview card showing exactly how customers will see your campaign, then click "Create Campaign".

Step C: Start Processing Transactions

1Click "New Transaction" on your dashboard

This is the big green button at the top of your dashboard.

2Enter the bill amount

Type the customer's bill amount (for amount campaigns) or just click to log a visit.

3Show QR to customer

A QR code will appear on your screen. Show it to the customer. They scan it with their phone camera.

4Customer confirms on WhatsApp

The customer enters their WhatsApp number (first time only — it remembers them afterwards!), taps confirm, and the transaction is logged automatically.

6. 🎯 Creating & Managing Campaigns

Campaign Types

💰 Spend & Win (Amount-Based)

Customers accumulate their total spending. Perfect for shops where order values vary — like sweet shops, restaurants, clothing stores.

Example: "Spend ₹5,000 in 30 days → Get a free box of sweets"

🏃 Visit & Win (Visit-Based)

Customers accumulate visit count. Perfect for businesses with fixed services — like salons, cafes, juice stalls.

Example: "Visit 10 times in 60 days → Get a free haircut"

Campaign Duration Options

  • Preset durations: 15, 30, 45, or 60 days
  • Specific end date: Pick an exact calendar date (e.g., Diwali, New Year)

Ending a Campaign

You can end an active campaign at any time from the Analytics page. When you end a campaign, all active enrollments are marked as expired, and you can create a new campaign immediately.

7. 💳 Processing Transactions

Making a Sale (Purchase)

  1. Open "New Transaction" from your dashboard
  2. Make sure "+ Purchase" is selected (green toggle)
  3. Enter the bill amount (for amount campaigns)
  4. Click "Generate QR Code"
  5. Show the QR to the customer — they scan it with their phone
  6. Wait for the screen to show "Transaction Recorded!"

Processing a Return / Refund

  1. Open "New Transaction"
  2. Switch to "− Return" (red toggle)
  3. Enter the refund amount
  4. Click "Generate Return QR"
  5. The QR will appear in red — customer scans it
  6. The refund amount is subtracted from their running total

⏱ QR Code Expiry

Every QR code expires in 3 minutes (180 seconds). If not scanned in time, it becomes invalid and you need to generate a new one. This is a security feature to prevent reuse.

Claiming Rewards

When a customer completes the goal, they receive a unique claim code on WhatsApp (e.g., "ABC1234"). They will come to your shop and show you the code. Go to your dashboard → "Winners Pending Claim" section → find their phone number and verify the code → click "Mark Claimed".

8. 📱 What Your Customers See

When a customer scans the QR code from their phone, they see a clean, simple page with:

  1. Your shop name and category
  2. Campaign details — goal description and reward
  3. Transaction amount being added
  4. WhatsApp number input (only for first-time customers)
  5. Countdown timer — showing how long until the QR expires

🔁 Returning Customers

If the customer has scanned before at your shop, the system automatically remembers their WhatsApp number. They see a "Welcome back!" message and are auto-confirmed within 3 seconds — no typing needed!

After confirming, the customer is redirected to WhatsApp where they tap "Send" to complete the process. They then receive their progress update automatically.

12. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do my customers need to download any app?

No! Customers only need WhatsApp, which they already have. They scan a QR code from their phone camera and everything happens through WhatsApp.

Q: What happens when a customer's campaign deadline passes?

Their enrollment is automatically marked as "expired". They can start fresh if you have an active campaign — they just need to scan the QR again.

Q: Can I run multiple campaigns at the same time?

Currently, you can have one active campaign at a time. End the current one before creating a new one.

Q: What if a customer asks for a refund?

Use the "Return" toggle on the New Transaction page. This subtracts the refund amount from the customer's running total.

Q: What happens if my subscription expires?

Your data is safe. Customers can't scan new QR codes, but all existing data remains. Contact us to renew.

Q: Can I upgrade my plan mid-month?

Yes! Contact the LoyaltyQR team and we'll upgrade your plan immediately. You'll get the new customer limit right away.

Q: Is the customer data safe?

Yes. All data is stored securely using Supabase (a Google Firebase-level platform). Only you and the LoyaltyQR admin can see your customer data.
